## Problem

The virtualizer key is the current array index, but the bounded history drops entries from its front. A retained index can then represent a different message while LogRow keeps local expanded state; measured row state can also follow the position.

## Evidence

Source trace: appendBounded slices from the front after capacity 5,000; getItemKey returns visible[index].index; LogRow owns useState(false). Populated logs were rendered, but the capacity-rollover interaction was not reproduced in this audit.

Source paths are relative to repository root. Line numbers refer to source revision 127dd90e0428ea0d90dfd77e48712e639d386d15; the two pre-existing firmware edits do not affect this evidence.

## Impact and severity rationale

Medium potential inspection error during long sessions: expanded details or visual measurements may transfer to another entry. Classified Probable pending a focused reproduction.

## Root cause

An array position is treated as permanent identity even though retention changes positions.

## Proposed resolution

Associate stable local receipt IDs with messages and use those for row identity, expansion and measurements without changing exported protocol payloads.

## Implementation record

None. Audit and planning only; no product code changed.

## Verification and regression evidence

Baseline evidence supports the finding, not its closure. Populate beyond capacity, expand one retained message, append more, filter and scroll. Expansion stays with that message, dropped messages disappear, and new messages start collapsed.
